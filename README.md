## 📌 About

This repo is where I track my personal journey through **Data Structures & Algorithms**, solving problems on [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/u/HabibTariq120/) to sharpen my problem-solving skills and build toward a career in software/AI engineering.

I'm a BSAI (Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence) student at the **University of Management and Technology (UMT), Lahore**, a **Google Developer Groups (GDG) Core Team Member** on campus, and a former **Peer Tutor for Programming Fundamentals**. This repo is part of how I keep those fundamentals sharp — one problem, committed and pushed, at a time.
